International warranty

Every timepiece that leaves our atelier carries the green seal — a promise that it has survived a battery of tests far beyond the industry norm, twice certified for precision on the wrist.

Cases are forged from a single block of Oystersteel, a grade 904L alloy exclusive to the manufacture. Bezels are cut, fluted and set by hand. Movements are assembled, regulated and disassembled again — then assembled once more, to perfection.

The result is a chronometer certified to −2/+2 seconds per day — a tolerance roughly twice as strict as the official standard for a certified chronometer.

“A watch does not merely keep time. It keeps a promise made to the second, for generations.”
— The Master Atelier, Geneva
